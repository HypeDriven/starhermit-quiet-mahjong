How to play

Goal

Remove matching pairs of free tiles until the table is empty.

Free tiles

A tile is free when no tile covers it and its left or right side is open. Free tiles glow softly.

Select

Tap or click a free tile, then tap a matching free tile. A second tap on the same tile deselects it.

Stuck

Hint shows a legal pair; Shuffle re-deals the remaining tiles. Both cost score.

Controls

Arrow keys move focus between free tiles, Enter selects, Esc pauses, H hint, U undo, S shuffle, C resets the camera.

Score

Pairs and streaks add points; hints, shuffles, undos and invalid taps subtract. Finishing under par adds a time bonus.
